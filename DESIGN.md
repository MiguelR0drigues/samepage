# Design System: SamePage

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Same Page, at a Glance"**

The dashboard is the logo made into a room: an ink-navy rail carrying the S symbol down the left, an ivory desk to its right, and white sheets laid on it. One colour, the coral of the logo's thread, is reserved for whatever is waiting on the person looking. Everything else is meant to be scanned rather than read: each kind of entry has its own hue and line icon, each person has a stable avatar colour, and every status is a dot with a word. The density sits between a ledger and a dashboard: cards carry real named things (a proposal title, a workstream name), never anonymous metrics.

The world is brand-led. Navy (#14213d), ivory (#f4f1ea) and coral (#ff6b4a) come straight from `docs/brand/` and are used as the product's own colours, not as accents on a neutral grey. One sans family carries every role; hierarchy is size and weight. Depth is a single soft navy-tinted shadow in light mode and a hairline border in dark mode. Motion is limited to 150ms colour fades.

The signature device is the division tally: one short tick per person, grouped by the position they took, the carried side in green, with the score written as "2–1". It appears beside every decision in timelines and at the head of a decision's Positions section.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Navy rail with the S symbol and an icon nav; ivory ground; white cards with a soft shadow.
- Coral only for what needs you, plus "you are here" markers.
- Colour-coded by kind (seven entry-type hues with tinted icon tiles) and by person (six avatar hues hashed from the person id).
- Status always as a dot chip: dot, word, 12% tint.
- One sans stack, weights 650/600/400, tabular figures for every time and count.
- Light and dark themes: follows `prefers-color-scheme` by default; a light / dark / system switch in the rail stores the choice in the `sp_theme` cookie and sets `data-theme` on `<html>`, which overrides the OS setting.

## Colors

A three-colour brand palette (navy, ivory, coral) does the structural work; a set of mid-saturation hues carries kind, person and status.

### Primary
- **Ink Navy** (navy, also rail / tx / btn): the rail background, all body text in light mode, the default filled button ("go"), and the hot focus tile. The active area link uses the go-button pair, so it flips to ivory on navy-night in dark mode. It is the brand's ink, so text is navy rather than black.
- **Page Ivory** (ivory, also bg / rail-tx / btn-tx): the page ground in light mode, rail text, text on navy, and all text in dark mode.
- **Thread Coral** (coral): the Accept button, the open-proposal count badge in the nav, the count inside a "hot" section heading, the hot tile's numeral and icon, the active nav item's icon, the verdict card's 4px top edge, the current node of a timeline, and the text caret. The focus ring uses the darker coral ink (#b8391e light, #ff8a70 dark) so it clears 3:1; on the navy rail it is coral. Text placed on coral is navy (5.67:1), never white.
- **Pressed Coral** (coral-hover): Accept button hover. It is a literal in the stylesheet, not a custom property.

### Secondary
- **Burnt Coral** (hot-ink) on **Coral Wash** (hot-soft): the "Proposed" half of a current-vs-proposed comparison and its label, and the text-selection colour. In dark mode they become #ff8a70 on #3a2026; the dark hot tile also uses Coral Wash with a coral border.
- **Pressed Navy** (btn-hi): hover for the navy "go" button. In dark mode the go button inverts to ivory and hovers to white.

### Tertiary
- **Entry-type hues**: Decision Blue (t-decision), Principle Teal (t-principle), View Violet (t-view), Exploration Amber (t-exploration), Proposal Brick (t-proposal), Workstream Green (t-workstream), Update Slate (t-update). Each drives a 34px icon tile (icon in the hue on a 13% tint) and the type word in meta lines. Dark mode lifts each to a lighter value (the `-dark` tokens).
- **Avatar hues** (av-0 to av-5: blue, brick, teal, violet, green, berry): white initials on a solid disc. The slot is a hash of the person id modulo 6, so a person keeps one colour on every page. These hues are not re-tuned for dark mode.
- **Status tones**: Carried Green (ok: active, accepted, done, unanimous), Majority Amber (warn: majority), Blocked Red (bad: blocked, no consensus, the "Blocked:" line on Team), Signal Blue (info: open, under review, in progress, the "Changed" marker, the New-since-visit tile), Retired Grey (off: superseded, rejected, removed, losing tally ticks).

### Neutral
- **Sheet White** (card): cards, inputs, default buttons, the active tab. Dark: Deep Navy Sheet (card-dark).
- **Warm Stone** (soft): area tags, section count pills, the tab track, workstream lanes, the "Current verdict" box. Dark: Navy Stone (soft-dark).
- **Ivory Rule** (line): 1px dividers between rows, input borders, the dashed empty-state border, the sticky top bar's bottom edge. Dark: Navy Rule (line-dark), which also borders every dark card.
- **Slate Ink** (mut): secondary text, meta lines, labels, placeholders, the search icon. Dark: mut-dark.
- **Rail Slate** (rail-mut) and **Rail Lift** (rail-hi): idle nav text on navy, and the translucent ivory fill behind hovered and active nav items.
- **Navy Mist** (navy-mut): secondary text on navy surfaces (the hot tile's caption, the active area's count). A literal in the stylesheet.

### Named Rules
**The Needs-You Coral Rule.** Coral marks only what is waiting on the viewer (Accept, the verdict count, the hot tile, a hot section count, the verdict edge) and where the viewer is (active nav icon, current timeline node, caret). It never decorates, never fills a large surface in light mode, and never carries body text on a light ground.

**The Kind-and-Person Rule.** Colour names a kind of entry or a person, nothing else. Entry types keep their fixed hue and icon; people keep their hashed avatar hue. A new surface reuses these assignments; it does not invent a new hue for emphasis.

**The Dot-and-Word Rule.** Status is never colour alone: a 6px dot, the status word, and a 12% tint of the tone, in a pill.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Inter (with "Segoe UI Variable Text", "Segoe UI", system-ui, -apple-system, Roboto, "Helvetica Neue", sans-serif)
**Body Font:** the same stack
**Label/Mono Font:** none; labels use the same stack

**Character:** A single humanist-neutral sans for chrome and record alike, so the colour system carries the scanning work and type stays quiet. Inter (the wordmark's face) is self-hosted: the variable latin and latin-ext subsets live in `public/fonts/` (OFL, licence alongside), served by the Worker's static assets, declared with `@font-face` (weight 100–900, `font-display: swap`) and the latin file preloaded. The system UI face is only the fallback while it loads.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (650, 28px, 1.2, -0.015em): page titles and the entry hero title; 23px below 760px.
- **Numeral** (700, 30px, 1, -0.02em, tabular): the count on each focus tile; 24px below 760px.
- **Headline** (650, 24px): the selected area's name on Who thinks what, capitalised.
- **Brand** (600, 18px, -0.02em): the SamePage wordmark beside the S symbol; 17px below 760px.
- **Section** (650, 17px): section headings, followed by a count pill and, on decisions, the tally.
- **Title** (650, 17px, 1.35): a proposal's title in its motion card. **Card title** (650, 16px): position names and person names on cards.
- **Verdict** (400, 17px, 1.6, max 70ch): the verdict text of a decision. **Lead** (400, 16.5px, 1.6, max 70ch): question and entry body text.
- **Body** (400, 15px, 1.55): default text. Feed titles use body size at 600; list and workstream titles use **Item title** (600, 14.5px).
- **Control** (600, 14px): buttons and tabs; inputs use 14px at 400.
- **Meta** (400, 13px, muted, tabular for times): by-lines, dates, area tags in context.
- **Label** (600, 13px, muted) and **Label small** (600, 12.5px, muted): day headers, "Current verdict" / "Proposed", timeline labels, field labels, "Working on:". Sentence case, no uppercase, no tracking.
- **Chip** (600, 12px, 18px line): status chips and type/kind tags. **Count** (700, 12px, 20px line): the coral nav badge.

### Named Rules
**The One Sans Rule.** One family, three working weights (650 headings, 600 titles and labels, 400 text; 700 only for numerals, counts and avatar initials). Hierarchy comes from size and weight, never from a second face.

**The Tabular Count Rule.** Every `time`, count, score and tile numeral uses tabular figures, so columns of counts and dates align.

## Layout

A two-column app shell: a 240px sticky navy rail and a fluid main column. The main column starts with a sticky top bar (12px by 40px padding, ivory, 1px bottom rule) holding the search group (icon, words, optional area select and a Search button joined into one control, capped at 760px). Page content sits in a centred container up to 1600px (`--maxw`) padded 30px top, 40px sides, 72px bottom; the top bar's inner row shares that width so search and content line up. Entry pages (decision, view, principle…) use the main + 340px aside split: the record on the left, a Details card (recorded, area, source, authors) and the History timeline on the right.

Content patterns, all reused across pages:
- **Head**: page title plus one muted sentence of status, 24px above the content.
- **Split**: main column plus a 340px aside (32px gap); the aside stacks panels 20px apart.
- **Focus row**: four equal tiles (14px gap).
- **Two**: a 240px sticky area rail (sticks at 84px) plus a content column (28px gap).
- **Auto-fill grids**: people (min 260px), crew (min 290px), sides (min 240px), all with 14px gaps.
- **Board**: three equal lanes on a Warm Stone track.
- Sections are 36px apart; section headings sit 14px above their content.

Responsive behaviour, from the stylesheet's three breakpoints:
- **Below 1180px**: the aside drops under the main column; focus tiles go to two columns.
- **Below 900px**: the area rail becomes a horizontal scrolling strip above the content; the board becomes one column.
- **Below 760px**: the rail becomes a top bar (wordmark, avatar and sign-out on one line, then a four-column icon-over-label nav with the coral count pinned to the icon); the top bar stops sticking; page padding drops to 20px / 16px / 56px; comparisons and Team label lines stack; the hero icon tile is hidden.

### Named Rules
**The Capped Growth Rule.** Anything that accumulates gets a limit or a way to narrow it from the first build: the feed shows 50 rows and then "…and N more", the Done lane shows 6 and folds the rest behind "Show N more", proposals get Open/Rejected tabs plus word and area filters, and views show one area at a time.

## Elevation & Depth

Hybrid, and theme-dependent. In light mode, cards are white with no border, lifted by one soft, navy-tinted two-layer shadow; the ivory ground does the rest of the separation. In dark mode the shadow token is `none` and every card gets a 1px Navy Rule border instead. Nothing else casts a shadow except the active tab (same token) and the 2px ring around stacked avatars, which uses the surface colour to cut overlapping discs apart.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Sheet** (`box-shadow: 0 1px 2px rgba(20,33,61,.06), 0 4px 14px rgba(20,33,61,.05)`): every card, focus tile and the active tab in light mode.
- **Avatar cut** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 2px var(--card)`, or `var(--bg)` in an entry hero): separates overlapping avatars in a stack.

### Named Rules
**The Soft Sheet Rule.** One elevation level only. Light mode lifts sheets with the single Sheet shadow and no border; dark mode removes the shadow and draws a hairline border. Never stack shadows, never use a hard offset shadow, never add a shadow to signal hover (hover darkens the border instead).

## Shapes

Softly rounded rectangles throughout, with radius stepping down as elements nest: cards, lanes, empty states and the hero icon tile at 14px; the tab track at 12px; controls, nav items, icon tiles, comparison boxes and workstream cards at 10px; tabs at 9px; area links and the sign-out button at 8px; area and kind tags at 6px (also the focus ring's radius). Fully round shapes are reserved for status chips, count badges and avatars (999px / 50%). Tally ticks are 2px-radius bars (3px when enlarged). Borders are 1px; the only heavier strokes are the 3px rule under each position card's header (green for the carried side) and the verdict card's 4px coral top edge. Empty states use a 1px dashed border.

## Components

### Buttons
Flat, solid and compact; colour says what the button does.
- **Shape:** gently rounded (10px), 40px minimum height, 8px by 15px padding, 600 weight 14px, 16px line icon with a 7px gap.
- **Hot (Accept):** Thread Coral fill and border, navy text. Used only for the action that resolves something waiting on you. Hover: Pressed Coral.
- **Go (primary):** navy fill, ivory text; hover Pressed Navy. In dark mode it inverts to an ivory fill with navy text and hovers to white.
- **Default:** Sheet White with a 1px Ivory Rule border and navy text (Search, Filter, Reject, Rename, Set status, Merge). Hover darkens the border to Slate Ink.
- **Hover / Focus:** 150ms fade on background and border colour; focus is the global 2px coral outline at 2px offset.

### Chips
- **Status chip:** pill, 600 12px on an 18px line, a 6px dot in the tone colour, the word in the tone colour, a 12% tint of the tone behind. Tones: ok, warn, bad, info, off.
- **Area tag:** 6px radius, Warm Stone fill, Slate Ink text, 500 12px. Areas are lowercase slugs shown as written.
- **Kind tag:** 6px radius, Signal Blue on a 12% tint ("Changed"), Retired Grey for "Removed".
- **Count pill:** in a section heading, Warm Stone with muted 600 12.5px; in a "hot" heading, coral with navy text.

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** 14px.
- **Background:** Sheet White (Deep Navy Sheet in dark).
- **Shadow Strategy:** the Sheet shadow in light, a 1px Navy Rule border in dark (see Elevation & Depth).
- **Border:** transparent 1px in light, so hover can tint it.
- **Internal Padding:** panels 18px by 20px; tiles, sides, columns and people cards 16-18px by 18-20px; rows inside a card 10-11px by 20px, divided by 1px rules.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** Sheet White, 1px Ivory Rule border, 10px radius, 8px by 12px padding, 40px minimum height, 14px text, muted placeholder. Hover darkens the border toward Slate Ink.
- **Focus:** the border turns Burnt Coral (hot-ink) with a 3px hot-ink halo at 20%.
- **Select:** a native `<select>`. Where `appearance: base-select` is supported (Chromium) the open list is styled: a card-coloured panel 6px below the control with a 12px radius, a soft drop shadow and 6px padding, fading in over 150ms; options are 8px by 12px rows with 8px radius, Warm Stone on hover or focus, Coral Wash plus a coral dot for the selected one; the chevron rotates 180° while open. Elsewhere it falls back to the OS list with the same closed look (inline SVG chevron, 38px right padding). Below 420px the top bar hides its area select so the words field keeps room.
- **Search group:** one 44px, 12px-radius white control holding the search icon, a borderless input, an optional area select behind a 1px divider, and a compact go button inset 4px. The whole group takes the focus ring (`:focus-within`). Used in the top bar and as the filter on Proposals and Who thinks what.

### Navigation
- **Rail:** navy, 240px, sticky full height. The S symbol (28px) and wordmark at the top; nav items as 18px line icon plus label, Rail Slate, 500 weight, 10px radius, 9px by 10px padding. Hover and active both fill with Rail Lift and switch to ivory text; the active item is 600 weight and its icon turns coral. The proposals item carries the coral count badge. The foot holds the theme switch (a three-button segmented control: sun, moon, monitor; the pressed one fills with Rail Lift and its icon turns coral, `aria-pressed` set) and, under a faint ivory rule, the signed-in person (avatar, name, sign-out icon button).
- **Tabs:** a Warm Stone track (12px radius, 4px padding); tabs are muted 600 14px with their count; the active tab is a white chip with the Sheet shadow.
- **Area rail:** a white card of area links with counts; the active link takes the go-button pair (navy with ivory text in light, ivory with navy text in dark).
- **Mobile:** see Layout; the rail becomes a top bar with a four-column icon-over-label nav.

### Entry Type Tile
A 34px rounded square (10px) holding the type's 17px line icon in the type hue on a 13% tint of it. It leads every feed row, search result, motion card and focus tile; the entry hero uses a 48px version (14px radius, 22px icon). Icons are Lucide-style, inlined as SVG on a 24px grid with a 2px round-capped stroke.

### Avatar
A disc with white 700 initials (first and last name): 32px default, 22px small (in meta lines and stacks), 44px large (Team crew). Stacks overlap by 3px with a 2px surface-coloured ring.

### Focus Tile
A white card (min height 136px, 16px by 18px padding) with an icon tile and a 30px tabular numeral on the top row, a 600 label, and a two-line muted caption that names what is counted (a proposal title, the kinds of change, the blocked workstream). Hover tints the border. The "hot" variant (when something needs your verdict) is filled navy with ivory text, a coral numeral and icon, and Navy Mist caption; in dark mode it becomes Coral Wash with a coral border.

### Motion (proposal card)
A card with a proposal type tile, the title, "Revises:" link, by-line, then a two-column comparison: "Current verdict" on Warm Stone and "Proposed" on Coral Wash with a Burnt Coral label (stacked below 760px). An optional bold "Why:" line follows; the footer, above a 1px rule, holds Accept (hot) and a "Reject…" disclosure that reveals a reason field, or a muted clock line when the viewer wrote the proposal.

### Day-Grouped Feed
Rows inside one card, grouped under muted 13px day labels ("today", "yesterday", "3 days ago", then "Earlier"). Each row: type tile, 600 title, meta line (avatar, author, type word in its hue, area tag, relative time), and a kind tag at the right when changed or removed. A removed entry shows only who removed what kind, never its title.

### Division Tally (signature)
One 18px by 3px tick per person, stacked per position group, groups side by side with 6px gaps; the carried group's ticks are Carried Green, the rest Retired Grey at 55%; then the score ("2–1", en dash, lone side "3–0") in muted 600 12px tabular. In a section heading the ticks grow to 34px by 6px and the score to 14px in text colour. The whole tally is one `role="img"` with an aria-label naming each position and its people. On a decision page each position becomes a card whose header has a 3px bottom rule, green for the carried side, and "N of M".

### Timeline
A vertical list with 10px ring nodes (2px Retired Grey border on the card colour) joined by a 2px Ivory Rule line; the current node ("This decision", or the newest progress update) is filled coral. Each item has a muted 600 13px label and a 600 title. Used for decision history and workstream progress.

### Workstream Board
Three Warm Stone lanes (In progress, Blocked, Done) headed by a status chip and a count; each workstream is a white 10px card with a 1px border, title, avatars and "N updates · when". Done is capped at six.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** reserve Thread Coral (#ff6b4a) for what needs the viewer and for "you are here" markers, and put navy text on it, never white.
- **Do** show status as a dot chip: 6px dot, the word, a 12% tint, pill-shaped.
- **Do** lead every entry with its type tile (type hue at 13% tint, Lucide-style 2px-stroke icon) and every person with their hashed avatar colour.
- **Do** make every count name what it counts: a focus tile's caption lists the actual titles or kinds behind its number.
- **Do** use the division tally wherever a decision's positions are summarised, with the carried side in Carried Green and the score as "2–1".
- **Do** set times, counts and scores in tabular figures.
- **Do** cap or filter anything that accumulates (feed 50, Done lane 6, tabs and filters on proposals, one area at a time on views).
- **Do** keep light-mode cards borderless with the Sheet shadow, and dark-mode cards shadowless with a 1px Navy Rule border.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use coral as decoration, as a large fill in light mode, or for body text on ivory or white.
- **Don't** convey status, consensus or kind by colour alone; pair it with a word, a dot or an icon.
- **Don't** introduce a second typeface or a serif; hierarchy is size and weight in the one sans stack.
- **Don't** add gradients, vanity metrics or anonymous KPI numbers; a tile without named contents does not ship.
- **Don't** stack shadows, use hard offset shadows, or add shadows on hover.
- **Don't** let a growing list render unbounded.
